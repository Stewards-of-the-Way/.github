# Stewards of the Way

**Serving Christ through Scripture, stewardship, and craftsmanship.**

> "Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me."  
> **John 14:6 (KJV)**

## Our Purpose

Stewards of the Way exists to honor Jesus Christ by building, preserving, and freely sharing biblical resources and ministry tools that help people study God's Word, understand Scripture, and serve the Church.

We believe the gifts and abilities entrusted to us are opportunities to serve others, not to bring recognition to ourselves.

Our work is rooted in the conviction that God's Word deserves faithful handling and that useful tools for studying and sharing it should be accessible across generations.

## What We Build

**Biblical Study Resources**

A growing library of Scripture translations, original-language tools, dictionaries, historical resources, cross-references, and biblical commentary, with attention to source accuracy and responsible attribution.

**Ministry Technology**

Reusable tools to help churches organize, preserve, search, and share biblical teaching through recordings, transcripts, Scripture connections, and other media.

**Connected Biblical Knowledge**

Structured resources that help people discover relationships among passages, historical contexts, theological concepts, and faithful biblical teaching.

## Our Stewardship

We seek to:

- Keep Jesus Christ central to the purpose of our work.
- Handle Scripture faithfully and distinguish biblical text from human interpretation.
- Respect the independence and responsibility of individual churches and ministries.
- Preserve reliable sources, responsible attribution, and appropriate usage rights.
- Build resources that others can maintain, improve, and carry forward.
- Make our tools and permitted resources freely accessible whenever possible.

## Why We Serve

> "As every man hath received the gift, even so minister the same one to another, as good stewards of the manifold grace of God."  
> **1 Peter 4:10 (KJV)**

We are not the source of the truth we share. We are servants entrusted with gifts, opportunities, and responsibilities.

**The work may pass from one generation to another. The purpose remains: to serve Christ and point others toward Him.**
